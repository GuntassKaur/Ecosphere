# Ecosphere
EcoSphere AI is an intelligent sustainability assistant that helps people track, understand, and reduce their carbon footprint through explainable analytics, personalized AI insights, peer benchmarking, and behavior-based recommendations.
Track Smarter. Understand Better. Reduce Faster.

EcoSphere AI is a sustainability intelligence platform designed to make carbon footprint tracking simple, understandable, and actionable.

Most carbon calculators stop after generating a score. Users see a number but rarely understand what it means or how to improve it.

EcoSphere AI focuses on the next step.

The platform combines a deterministic carbon calculation engine with AI-powered analysis to help users identify emission patterns, compare themselves with regional benchmarks, discover improvement opportunities, and simulate the impact of lifestyle changes before making them.

Our goal is not only to measure environmental impact but to help people change it.

🚨 The Problem

Many existing carbon footprint tools suffer from three major issues:

They provide scores without meaningful explanations.
They offer generic recommendations that are not personalized.
They lack continuous tracking and behavior-based insights.

As a result, users often abandon these platforms after a single use because they do not see ongoing value.

💡 Our Solution

EcoSphere AI transforms carbon tracking into an ongoing sustainability experience.

Instead of asking:

"What is my carbon footprint?"

EcoSphere helps users answer:

"Why is my footprint changing?"

"Which habit contributes the most?"

"How do I improve it?"

"What happens if I change my behavior?"

⭐ Key Features
EcoSphere Score™

A transparent sustainability score that combines multiple emission categories into a simple and understandable rating system.

Includes:

Score out of 100
Grade (A–F)
Monthly trend
Biggest impact area
Fastest improvement opportunity
Explainable Carbon Engine

Unlike many AI-only approaches, all carbon calculations are performed using deterministic emission factors and mathematical models.

This ensures:

Accuracy
Transparency
Consistency
Trustworthiness
AI Sustainability Coach

Users can interact with an AI coach that understands:

Current EcoSphere Score
Historical activity
Benchmark comparisons
Active anomalies

The coach provides personalized sustainability guidance instead of generic advice.

AI Anomaly Detection

EcoSphere continuously analyzes user activity and identifies unusual emission spikes.

Example:

Your transport emissions increased by 42% compared to your typical weekly pattern.

This allows users to discover issues they might otherwise miss.

Peer Benchmarking

Users can compare their emissions with regional averages.

Example:

Transport vs Regional Average
Electricity vs Regional Average
Food vs Regional Average

This creates context around personal sustainability performance.

What-If Simulator

An interactive simulation engine that allows users to test lifestyle changes before making them.

Examples:

Switching from car to public transport
Reducing electricity usage
Changing dietary habits

Users immediately see projected emission reductions.

Weekly Sustainability Log

A lightweight tracking system that allows users to record changes in their lifestyle and monitor long-term progress.

LinkedIn Progress Generator

Automatically generates professional sustainability progress posts based on user achievements and improvements.

This feature was inspired by the PromptWars submission process itself.

Live Carbon Intensity Widget

Displays real-time electricity grid carbon intensity information and helps users understand how energy consumption impacts emissions.

🏗️ Architecture

EcoSphere AI follows a hybrid architecture.

Carbon Intelligence Layer

Responsible for:

Carbon calculations
EcoSphere Score generation
Benchmark comparisons
Emission breakdowns
AI Intelligence Layer

Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash.

Responsible for:

Sustainability coaching
Insight generation
Anomaly explanations
LinkedIn content generation

This separation ensures that AI enhances decision-making without being responsible for mathematical calculations.
